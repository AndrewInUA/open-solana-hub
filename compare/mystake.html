How to read this
  • ✅ OK – Everything looks fine. You don’t need to do anything right now.
  • 👀 Watch – Something’s a bit off – worth a look, not an emergency.
  • ⚠️ Risk – This needs attention before you decide anything. Open the validator profile.
  • Stake accounts with no validator are leftovers – they are not earning. They do not change the health label at the top.
  • Last epoch is the latest payout. Extra payouts are a consecutive run we could follow – not lifetime history. Your validator is this operator’s profile: voting, stability, and fee history.

Native stake only. Liquid-staking tokens do not appear here. Connecting a wallet only reads the public key – nothing is signed except the wallet’s own connect prompt, and we never move SOL.

Sources. Wallet lookups use the Validator Transparency /api/my-stake endpoint (Helius-backed stake scan + last finished epoch via getInflationReward). A single stake account (or a downed API) falls back to public Solana RPC – some browsers block that RPC, so the wallet field is the reliable path. Extra recent payouts appear only when further getInflationReward calls return amounts. Health overlays use the same /api/rpc, /api/ratings, and /api/snapshots feeds as the compare dashboard (Your validator). Approximate fiat uses CoinGecko (SOL price) with an exchangerate-api fallback; rates are cached about 15 minutes and sit next to Approx. / ≈ amounts, never under Signals. Existing ?vote= links are unchanged.
